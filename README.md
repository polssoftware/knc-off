# KNC OFF
KNC OFF - Drums that SMAK! Made with AI.
Process your drums to make them slam, bang, wreck, rumble, clobber, or SMAK! Anything but that one word. 

SMAK - Transient Shaper
SAT - Saturation
SUB - Sub Bass
AIR - High frequency exciter
CLIP - Clipper (Soft/Hard)
COMP - Compressor
SHFT - Pitch Shifter
BIT - Bitcrusher

HP - High Pass
LP - Low Pass

DLY - Delay
RVB - Reverb
CHR - Chorus
WID - Width

L - Lows
M - Mids
H - Highs

Comes with 50 presets and a randomize button.

Hold shift to change knobs/sliders at whole numbers
Hold shift while clicking in the presets menu to keep menu open
Double click knob/sliders to reset to default state
Click the bracket above DLY, RVB, CHR to open menu
Click and drag the main knobs (top 8 knobs) to rearrange order
Click and drag the fx knobs (bottom 4 knobs) to rearrange order
Click and drag bottom right to resize GUI
Top right is bypass, next to it is light/dark mode toggle

GUI is drawn in paint
